## 2. Product Features -- Complete Inventory

### 2.1 Market Information

- **Market Summary**: Real-time overview of KOSPI/KOSDAQ trends
- **Featured Stocks (특징종목)**: Sharp risers, volume surges, new highs/lows, reported prices
  - 2025 update: Now supports international stocks and cryptocurrency in featured stocks
- **Market Indicators**: KOSPI, KOSDAQ, overseas indices, forex, commodities, interest rates
  - 2025 update: Users can pin favorite indicators to persistent bottom bar
- **Market Issues**: Curated news and market-moving events

### 2.2 Stock Analysis

- **Stock Summary**: Company overview, performance metrics, investment highlights
- **Financial Statements**: Income statements, balance sheets, financial ratios, debt ratios
- **Stock-related News & Reports**: Aggregated news per ticker
- **Theme Stocks**: Categorized by investment themes

### 2.3 AI & Algorithmic Features

- **AI Price Prediction (AI 예측)**: AI-generated expected stock prices and investment opinions per ticker
  - Methodology: Not publicly disclosed in detail
  - No publicly available accuracy/performance metrics
  - Available to Premium subscribers
- **Trading Signals (매매신호)**: Daily buy/sell opportunities
  - Runs 340,000+ simulations every 10 minutes (as of June 2025 renewal)
  - Provides cumulative profit rate graphs
  - Pro tier: 10 signals, domestic stocks only
  - Premium tier: 20 signals, includes international and crypto
- **Technical Indicator Analysis (지표분석)**:
  - Supports 43 trading strategies for comparison
  - Automatic interpretation of results (simplified in April 2025 redesign)
  - "Next signal" notification system
  - Trade timing overlays on charts
  - 7-day free trial available

### 2.4 Quantitative Tools

- **Backtesting**:
  - Uses 10+ years of historical data for all listed stocks
  - 4-step process: Stock Selection -> Trading Settings -> Strategy Creation -> Results Review
  - Stock selection: Direct (up to 50) or condition-based filtering (market, valuation indicators)
  - Algorithm options: Single conditions, grouped conditions, or pre-made templates
  - Rebalancing: Automatic portfolio adjustment at specified intervals
  - Cost: Uses "seeds" (virtual currency) -- 1 seed for <=2 years, up to 5 seeds for 8+ years
  - Failed tests auto-retry 3x with seed refund
- **Stock Filter**: Condition-based stock screening by market, valuation metrics, custom criteria

### 2.5 Gamification

- **Chart Game (차트게임)**:
  - Historical chart simulation for risk-free trading practice
  - 50 turns per game with buy/sell/skip decisions
  - Used for S-MAT (Korea Securities Personnel Development Institute) practical exams
  - **Free tier**: 300 historical candles, basic moving averages/volume
  - **Premium tier** (19,800 KRW/month):
    - 8 chart indicators + 37 supplementary indicators
    - Trading strategy signal overlays
    - Turn extension (25 turns x 2 per game, costs 1 seed)
    - Chart switching (max 2 per game)
    - 900 historical candles (3x free)
    - 5 separate accounts for strategy testing
    - Multi-market: domestic, US stocks, crypto
    - Minute-based timeframes (1/3/5/10/15/30/60-min)
    - Game review/replay with turn-by-turn analysis
  - **Friend Battles**: Challenge friends with referral/sharing features (Sept 2025 update)
  - **Investment Leagues**: Ranked competitions over a period, cumulative P&L
  - **Alpha Chart** (international version): Available in 52 countries, attracted ~10,000 users within 2 months of launch
  - Sept 2025: Added timeline sharing, review memos, trade history notes

### 2.6 Trading Execution

- **Paper Trading**: Available without certificate authentication
- **Real Trading (실거래)**:
  - Partner brokers: **Uanta Securities (유안타증권)** and **KB Securities (KB증권)**
  - Commission: 0.015% per trade
  - New user benefit: 3-month commission-free trial
  - KB Securities integration: Mobile-only for trading execution; portfolio/history viewable on all devices
  - Feb 2025 update: Real execution data visibility, position holding status, order book design updates

### 2.7 Community & Content

- **Timeline Feed**: Real-time investment posts shared by users
- **Stock Prediction**: Community participation in stock predictions
- **Partner Signals (파트너 신호)**: Professional trading strategies from expert contributors (launched May 2025)
  - Free trial: 2 weeks for first 1,000 users
  - Features content from notable traders (e.g., super-ant trader 전태룡)
- **Telegram Market Analysis**: Free real-time market analysis from professional contributors (launched April 2025)

### 2.8 Platform & Technical

- **Architecture**: Cloud-based SaaS (Web Trading System / WTS)
- **Cross-platform**: Web (Chrome), macOS app, Windows, iOS, Android, tablet, browser extensions
- **Single login**: All devices synced in real-time, no digital certificate required
- **Three-panel layout**: Watchlist + Chart + Smart tools simultaneously visible

### 2.9 Other Features

- **Portfolio Monitoring**: Track holdings across connected brokers
- **Seeds (virtual currency)**: Used for backtesting, chart game extensions; purchasable (2,200 / 9,900 / 17,000 KRW)
- **Affiliate Program** (launched Dec 2024): 30% revenue share on referred sales, immediate payout
- **Friend Referral**: 30 seeds for inviter + invitee (Oct 2025)

## 4. User Reviews & Complaints -- Aggregated Findings

### What Users Love

1. **Clean, intuitive UI/UX**: Consistently praised as superior to traditional HTS/MTS platforms. One App Store reviewer: "단연 최고입니다" (definitely the best)
2. **Mac compatibility**: One of the few Korean investment platforms that works natively on macOS
3. **Cross-device sync**: Seamless transition between mobile, tablet, and PC
4. **No certificate hassle**: No digital certificate (공인인증서) required
5. **All-in-one dashboard**: Multiple data types (charts, watchlists, financials) visible simultaneously
6. **Active development**: Users commend responsive dev team and frequent updates
7. **Chart Game**: Unique gamification for learning technical analysis
8. **iPad optimization**: Smooth tablet experience

### What Users Complain About

1. **Limited international stock data**: Real-time overseas market data historically missing or limited; dev team cited high data licensing costs as barrier
2. **No dollar-denominated charts**: Charts only available in KRW-base; users want USD-base option
3. **Missing widgets**: No home screen widget support
4. **No dark mode** (historical request; unclear if resolved)
5. **Limited search in market issues**: No keyword search within market news section
6. **UI customization requests**: Users want swipeable watchlists, adjustable text sizes
7. **Supply/demand traders underserved**: Chart-centric layout doesn't serve order-flow/institutional-flow traders well
8. **No industry peer comparison**: Users want same-sector stock comparison features
9. **Small team limitations**: With only 11-15 employees, feature velocity is constrained
10. **Premium pricing perception**: Monthly subscriptions (up to 69,900 KRW) perceived as expensive for individual features

### Feature Requests (Unfulfilled as of Research Date)

- Smartwatch support
- US App Store availability
- Advanced order types (credit trading, short selling clarity)
- Multi-chart view capability
- Price alerts (unclear if fully implemented)

## 6. Competitive Landscape

### Market Context

- Korean fintech market size (2025): $4.17B, growing at 7.3% CAGR to $7.86B by 2034
- AlphaSquare competes in the **investment information & analysis** segment, not pure brokerage

### Direct Competitors

#### Tier 1: Major Securities App (MTS) -- Brokerage-First

| App | MAU | UX Score | Key Strength | Key Weakness |
|-----|-----|----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Mirae Asset m.stock** | #1 by users | 67.2 | Fast, lightweight, multiple order screens | Unintuitive navigation |
| **Kiwoom YoungmunS** | 3.43M | Below avg | Most advanced charting/screening, #1 market share | Slow loading, dated UI |
| **Samsung mPop** | 2.84M | Moderate | Institutional/foreign flow data, clean interface | Separated CMA/trading accounts |
| **Korea Investment MTS** | 2.65M | Moderate | Widget-based, prediction displays | Slow, server stability issues |
| **KB Securities M-able** | 2.64M | Moderate | Strong brand, broad services | Generic experience |
| **Namu (NH Investment)** | 2.42M | 68.4 | Modern design, Kakao Bank integration | Weak research, low offering score |

#### Tier 2: Information-First Platforms -- Direct Competitors

| App | Users | Key Strength | Key Weakness |
|-----|-------|-------------|-------------|
| **Toss Securities (토스증권)** | Massive (Toss ecosystem) | #1 UX score (73.2), beginner-friendly, integrated ecosystem | Less depth for advanced traders |
| **Securities Plus (증권플러스)** | DAU 360K (2019), rising rapidly | Multi-broker aggregation, Kakao integration, dark mode, alerts | Aging data; Dunamu ownership complexity |
| **AlphaSquare** | 220K registered, 120K MAU | Best cross-platform, gamification, AI signals, backtesting | Small scale, limited overseas data |
| **Naver Finance** | Very large (web-dominant) | News integration, broad coverage, free | No trading, no advanced analysis |

### AlphaSquare's Competitive Position

**Strengths vs. Competitors:**

1. **Only true WTS (Web Trading System)**: Works on macOS, any browser, any device -- unique in Korean market where most platforms require Windows HTS or mobile MTS
2. **Gamification leadership**: Chart Game + Investment Leagues have no direct equivalent in Korean securities apps
3. **Modern UI/UX**: Consistently rated higher than traditional HTS/MTS for design clarity
4. **AI/quantitative tools accessible to retail**: Backtesting, AI predictions, 43-strategy comparison at relatively low price points
5. **No digital certificate requirement**: Frictionless onboarding

**Weaknesses vs. Competitors:**

1. **Tiny scale**: 220K users vs. millions for Kiwoom/Samsung/Toss
2. **Limited broker integration**: Only 2 brokers (Uanta, KB) vs. each MTS being its own broker
3. **Revenue still minimal**: Sub-1B KRW revenue vs. profitable incumbents
4. **Team size**: 11-15 people cannot match feature velocity of larger teams
5. **Data costs**: Cannot afford real-time international data that larger platforms offer free
6. **No ecosystem moat**: Toss has super-app, Kakao has messaging, Naver has search -- AlphaSquare has no adjacent platform leverage
7. **Brand awareness**: Virtually unknown outside tech-savvy early adopters

## 7. Strategic Analysis

### AlphaSquare's Growth Strategy (from CEO interview + 2025 updates)

1. **Content monetization**: Partner Signals marketplace, expert trader content (전태룡 partnership)
2. **B2B partnerships**: Korea Economic TV partnership for AlphaPick (미국주식 매매타이밍 서비스)
3. **Broker expansion**: Currently Uanta + KB; plan to add more
4. **International expansion**: Alpha Chart in 52 countries; planned entry into North America, Europe, Southeast Asia
5. **Platform stickiness**: Gamification (Chart Game) as user acquisition funnel -> conversion to paid analysis tools
6. **Affiliate program**: 30% revenue share to incentivize growth

### Key Risks

1. **Cash runway**: ~4.1B KRW total raised with 15 employees; burn rate likely high relative to <1B KRW revenue
2. **Broker dependency**: Real trading revenue depends entirely on 2 broker partnerships
3. **AI credibility**: No public accuracy metrics for AI predictions; could face regulatory/trust issues
4. **Competition from giants**: Toss, Kakao, Naver can build similar features with 100x resources
5. **Regulation**: Korean financial regulation is strict; platform changes need compliance review

### What AlphaSquare Gets Right That Incumbents Miss

1. **Cross-platform web-first**: Incumbents are Windows HTS + mobile MTS silos
2. **Retail quantitative tools**: Backtesting and AI signals at accessible price points
3. **Learning through play**: Chart Game is genuinely innovative for investor education
4. **Clean modern UX**: Traditional HTS platforms look like they were designed in 2005
5. **Integrated workflow**: Information -> Analysis -> Paper trade -> Real trade in one platform

### What AlphaSquare Misses Entirely

1. **Macro/environmental analysis**: No systematic framework for macro regime analysis, geopolitical scanning, or multi-factor environmental assessment
2. **Signal lifecycle tracking**: No way to see how a signal evolved, why it was generated, when it changed, or its historical accuracy
3. **Multi-language intelligence**: Korean-only; no global news in English/Chinese/Japanese that affects Korean markets
4. **Narrative/thesis building**: No tools for building and tracking investment theses over time
5. **Portfolio-level risk analysis**: Individual stock focus; no portfolio correlation, VaR, or scenario stress testing
6. **Tax and regulatory intelligence**: No automated tax optimization or regulatory change alerts
7. **Alternative data**: No satellite data, credit card spending, web traffic, or other alternative data sources
8. **Institutional-grade depth**: Order flow analysis, dark pool data, options chain analysis all absent
