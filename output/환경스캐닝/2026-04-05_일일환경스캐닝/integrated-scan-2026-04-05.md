### 8.3 FSSF Type Definitions
- **Weak Signal**: Early-stage, limited evidence, high uncertainty, potentially transformative
- **Emerging Issue**: Growing evidence base, medium certainty
- **Trend**: Clear directional movement with strong evidence
- **Megatrend**: Long-term, structural, high-certainty macro force
- **Driver**: Active forcing function causing other changes
- **Wild Card**: Low probability but extremely high impact
- **Discontinuity**: Abrupt regime break — past trajectories no longer predictive
- **Precursor Event**: Enables or triggers subsequent significant change

### 8.4 Three Horizons Framework
- **H1 (0-2 years)**: Changes within current paradigm
- **H2 (2-7 years)**: Transitional signals; emerging alternatives
- **H3 (7+ years)**: Seeds of future paradigms
