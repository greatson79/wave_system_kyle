# CMT / FCF / HP Rules

## Priority

These rules have highest priority for CMT, FCF, and HP outputs.

## CMT

- Derive the Subject from the passage boundary.
- Make the Complement concrete; avoid vague abstraction.
- Write the CMT as exactly one sentence.
- Preserve gospel-centered logic.

## FCF

- Do not reduce FCF to a moral flaw.
- Reveal the need for the gospel.
- Focus on idols, distorted desires, fears, or self-salvation attempts.
- Write one or two sentences.

## HP

- Make HP answer the FCF through the CMT.
- Write HP as one sentence that leads into actual life.
- Use religious language by default.
- Provide a non-Christian-friendly neutral version only when requested.

## Integrity Checks

- CMT must answer the passage, not a favorite doctrine.
- FCF must expose need, not shame the audience.
- HP must flow from gospel logic, not moral pressure.
