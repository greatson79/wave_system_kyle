# Four Operating Principles

Preference: Core work in this Codex folder must follow thinking before coding, simplicity first, surgical changes, and goal-driven execution.
Preference: If intent, source of truth, or success criteria are ambiguous, stop and ask instead of guessing.
Preference: Avoid speculative extensibility, unnecessary abstraction, and broad frameworks; choose the smallest clear solution.
Preference: Keep edits surgical and do not touch working functions, comments, formatting, or nearby code for cleanup without reporting the improvement first.
Preference: When success criteria, a reproduction test, or a characterization check exists, iterate and re-run verification until it passes or a concrete blocker is found.
