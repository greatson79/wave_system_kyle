## Procedure

1. Confirm the passage boundary and requested output scope.
2. Use only the passage and data supplied by the user unless a source is verified.
3. Identify the passage's central message.
4. Describe its redemptive-historical location without forcing allegory.
5. Note historical, cultural, and theological observations.
6. Summarize normative, existential, and situational angles.
7. Mark uncertain claims as `확인 필요`.
