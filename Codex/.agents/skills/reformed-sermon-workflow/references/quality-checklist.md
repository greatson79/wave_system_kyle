# Quality Checklist

Use this before finalizing sermon-preparation output.

- CMT -> FCF -> HP connection is logically clear.
- Christ's representative work is explicit.
- The output has not become moralism.
- The interpretation does not generalize beyond the passage.
- Uncertain information is separated as `확인 필요`.
- Direct theologian quotations are not invented.
- Original-language claims are not speculative.
- The requested scope is preserved.
- The output is structured and concise unless a full manuscript is requested.
