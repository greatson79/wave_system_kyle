# Sermon Framework

## Stage 1: 본문 연구 및 분석

Analyze the passage from historical, cultural, and theological angles within Reformed and evangelical boundaries.

Questions:

- What is the central message of the passage?
- Where does this passage stand in redemptive history?
- What does it show normatively, existentially, and situationally?

## Stage 1.5: FCF - 인간의 타락상

Identify the fundamental human distortion, idol, disordered desire, fear, or self-salvation attempt exposed by the passage.

## Stage 2: CMT

Define:

- Subject: concise passage-bound topic.
- Complement: one or two concrete statements completing the subject.
- CMT Sentence: one complete sentence combining subject and complement.

## Stage 3: Moral Principle

State the ethical or practical demand honestly derived from the text.

## Stage 4: Human Effort Failure

Show why humans cannot fulfill the demand by self-effort. Dismantle self-salvation narratives.

## Stage 5: 그리스도의 인격과 사역

Show how the passage is fulfilled in Christ. Keep the focus on representative obedience and the cross.

## Stage 6: Hermeneutical Points

Connect CMT and FCF to contemporary cultural narratives and practical application.

## Stage 7: 복음 적용

Apply the gospel concretely to personal and communal life.
